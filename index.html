Before we talk

A few useful
starting points.

Something else you’d like to know?
Ask Paula →

Which areas do you cover?

CAWS has its roots in Leicestershire. Share your school’s location with Paula to check current coverage and availability.

Can we discuss a particular attendance concern?

Yes. Contact Paula with a general outline of the support your school needs. Please leave out pupil names and sensitive case details in an initial enquiry.

Do you work directly with families?

Support includes parent meetings in school, home visits and liaison with other professionals. Discuss your school’s needs with Paula to agree the appropriate approach.

How is support arranged and priced?

Contact Paula to discuss your priorities, the support required, availability and costs before making any arrangements.

I’m a parent. Where should I start?

For a concern about your own child’s attendance, speak to your school’s attendance lead first. They can explain the support available and how CAWS may be involved.
